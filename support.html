Zenith: Night Sky

Questions, bugs or ideas: open a support request and it will be answered there.

Tips

  • The sky does not follow my phone. Make sure Follow phone is on in More. If the compass is off, move your phone in a figure eight.
  • The camera view does not match the sky. Drag with two fingers to nudge the alignment.
  • I want to fly to the planets. Turn Gravity off (top left). Open More and choose Go to.
  • Satellites look out of date. Satellite positions need fresh data. Turn Satellites on with an internet connection once a day.
  • Faint stars are hard to see outside. Turn on Night mode in More and give your eyes about 30 minutes in the dark.
